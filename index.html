Case studies

Saving a product its biggest clients were ready to leave

Policy Manager is where companies like Salesforce and Cisco, and large US school districts, write and approve their compliance policies. When Diligent acquired it, it crashed often and didn't follow the design system, and some of the largest clients were threatening to leave.

As the sole product designer, I made failures safe first, then restructured the review cycle and integrated Word and Google Docs.

Taking film production finance out of spreadsheets

Conduiit is a cloud platform where productions for Amazon, Netflix, HBO and Discovery manage purchase orders, payment requests and approvals. Before it, teams juggled email, spreadsheets and shared folders.

I designed the UX/UI of the product: digital forms, an approval matrix for approval chains and limits, cost report dashboards, and checks that flag miscoded submissions and missing files before an audit. Each studio can apply its own branding to the interface.

Sole UX/UI designer · Web

Bringing bill payments back to Edelap’s own app

Edelap’s virtual office was so confusing that many customers paid their electricity bill through third-party wallets instead. I audited it with heatmaps, card sorting and interviews, then redesigned it as a mobile app where paying directly is the first thing people see.

Designing for the platform 15,000 Mercado Libre engineers ship with

Fury is the platform Mercado Libre’s 15,000+ engineers use to deploy and monitor more than 30,000 microservices. I designed new features and UX fixes with the squads that own them, using Scotia, our version of Andes for logs, metrics and dense tables.

When Fury started selling to other companies, I worked on a modular version where clients pay only for the features they use.
