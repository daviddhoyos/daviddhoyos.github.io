Saving a product its biggest clients were ready to leave

Policy Manager is where companies like Salesforce and Cisco, and large US school districts, write and approve their compliance policies. When Diligent acquired it, it crashed often and didn't follow the design system, and some of the largest clients were threatening to leave.

As the sole product designer, I made failures safe first, then restructured the review cycle and integrated Word and Google Docs.

Film Production Finance

Conduiit is a cloud platform where productions for studios like Netflix, HBO and Starz manage purchase orders, payment requests and approvals. Before it, teams juggled email, spreadsheets and shared folders.

I designed the UX/UI of the product: digital forms, an approval matrix for approval chains and limits, cost report dashboards, and checks that flag miscoded submissions and missing files before an audit. Each studio can apply its own branding to the interface.

Scaling B2B Cloud SaaS

Mercado Libre's internal developer platform started selling its cloud infrastructure to other companies.

Working with engineering and PMs, I owned the UX of key flows like deployment pipelines and execution tracking. The screens had to work both for engineers who know the platform and for business users who don't.

Using AI for rapid prototyping, our team transformed raw cloud complexity into a user-friendly product, leading to fast adoption and positive feedback from new B2B clients.
